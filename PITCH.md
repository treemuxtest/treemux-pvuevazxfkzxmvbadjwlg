This project turns a familiar topic into a cinematic data story: the solar system reframed through five surprising perspectives on distance, size, light, and time. As users scroll, the same dataset transforms into new visual models, creating clear “aha” moments that make planetary scale emotionally legible instead of abstract. Hover and click interactions invite exploration without breaking flow, so the experience feels more like a digital feature story than a chart. The design leans on restrained typography, atmospheric color, and buttery canvas animation to feel premium from the first frame.
